Topic 01 • Core Definitions

What Are Research & Research Skills?

🔬 Definition of Research

A careful, step-by-step scientific investigation to discover new facts, solve clinical problems, and verify what works best for patients.

🧠 Definition of Research Skills

Practical abilities to ask clear clinical questions, find reliable scientific data, judge its quality, and design an ethical study.

Topic 02 • Data Classification

Types of Research Data (Two Separate Classifications)

Data is classified in two completely different ways—by where it comes from and by what form it takes:

A. By Source (Who collected it?)

  • Primary Data (First-Hand): Collected directly by you.
    Example: Surveying 60 students on hand hygiene.
  • Secondary Data (Existing): Already collected by others.
    Example: Reading hospital infection logs.

B. By Nature (Numbers or Words?)

  • Quantitative (Numbers): Measured in digits.
    Example: Blood pressure (120/80 mmHg).
  • Qualitative (Words): Feelings & experiences.
    Example: Interviewing stressed ICU nurses.

C

A

Practical Example: A blog claiming "Herbal tea cures diabetes in 2 days" with no author and a "Buy Now" button fails Authority, Accuracy, and Purpose.

Subtopic 23 • Smart Search Techniques

Refining Searches with Boolean Operators

Type AND, OR, NOT in CAPITAL letters to control your database search results:

AND (Narrows)

Nursing
Safety

Nursing AND Patient Safety
Finds articles with both terms.

OR (Broadens)

Nurse
Nursing

Nurse OR Nursing
Finds articles with either term.

NOT (Excludes)

Infection
COVID

Infection NOT COVID-19
Removes unwanted topics.

Topic 07 • Study Methodology

Comparing Research Designs

A research design is your overall plan for collecting and analyzing data:

Research Design Main Purpose Type of Data Nursing Example
1. Quantitative Measure variables & test cause-and-effect Numbers & stats Counting students following handwashing steps
2. Qualitative Understand human feelings & lived experiences Words & stories Interviewing nurses about ICU stress
3. Mixed-Methods Get complete picture using both approaches Numbers & words Exam scores (numbers) + student feedback (words)

Subtopic 24 • Step-by-Step Guide

Steps in Developing a Research Proposal

  • Step 1 — Identify Problem: Notice real ward issue (e.g., low hand hygiene).
  • Step 2 — Review Literature: Search PubMed & CINAHL for existing studies.
  • Step 3 — Formulate Objectives: Choose study design and questionnaire.
  • Step 4 — Plan Ethics & Timeline: Prepare consent forms and work schedule.
  • Step 5 — Submit Approval: Get clearance from supervisor before starting.
Careful planning prevents mistakes during data collection
Topic 09 • Protecting Participants

Ethical Principles in Nursing Research

🤝 Informed Consent

Explain risks and benefits clearly. Participants must join freely and can stop anytime without penalty.

🔒 Confidentiality

Never publish patient names or IDs. Use secret codes (e.g., Student #01) to keep personal records private.

⚖️ Beneficence & Justice

Do Good, Do No Harm, and treat every participant fairly and equitably.

📝 Proper Referencing

Never plagiarize. Always write in your own words and cite original sources properly.
