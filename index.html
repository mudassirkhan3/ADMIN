Core Foundations • Definitions & Nursing Relevance

Definitions & Why Research Skills Matter in Nursing

🔬 Definition of Research

A systematic, scientific investigation carried out step-by-step to discover new facts, verify clinical knowledge, and solve healthcare problems.

🧠 Definition of Research Skills

The practical ability to ask focused clinical questions, search trusted scientific databases, evaluate evidence quality, and design an ethical study.

🩺 Why Research Skills Are Essential in Nursing:

  • Ensures Patient Safety: Replaces outdated ward habits with scientifically proven nursing care.
  • Strengthens Critical Thinking: Helps nurses verify drug protocols, wound care, and infection control.
  • Supports Evidence-Based Practice (EBP): Connects classroom science directly to bedside decisions.
  • Improves Recovery Outcomes: Reduces hospital readmissions and clinical complications.
Main Topic 1 • Competency 23 • Data Classification

Types of Research Data (Two Separate Classifications)

Data is classified in two completely different ways—by where it comes from and by what form it takes:

A. By Source (Who collected it?)

  • Primary Data (First-Hand): Collected directly by you.
    Example: Surveying 60 students on hand hygiene.
  • Secondary Data (Existing): Already collected by others.
    Example: Reading hospital infection logs.

B. By Nature (Numbers or Words?)

  • Quantitative (Numbers): Measured in digits.
    Example: Blood pressure (120/80 mmHg).
  • Qualitative (Words): Feelings & experiences.
    Example: Interviewing stressed ICU nurses.
Main Topic 1 • Competency 23 • Locate Credible Scientific Data

Reliable Scientific Databases for Nursing Literature

Avoid unverified blogs or social media posts. Use indexed health databases—and always critically appraise individual search results:

1. PubMed

36M+ biomedical & clinical articles.

2. CINAHL

Premier nursing & allied health index.

3. Cochrane Library

Gold standard for systematic reviews.

4. Google Scholar

Broad scholarly search (verify peer-review).

Database Credibility Filter COMPETENCY 23
✅ CINAHL & PubMed Peer-Reviewed Journals
⚠️ Google Scholar Preprints (Verify Peer Review)
❌ Unverified Social Media & Commercial Blogs
Always verify source credibility before applying to patient care
Main Topic 1 • Competency 23 • Evaluating Source Credibility

The CRAAP Test: Five Checkpoints for Credibility

C

Currency

Recent? (<5 yrs)

R

Relevance

Fits topic?

A

Authority

Expert author?

A

Accuracy

Backed by data?

P

Purpose

Science or sales?

Practical Outcome: A peer-reviewed CINAHL article passes all five checks, whereas a commercial blog claiming "Herbal tea cures diabetes in 2 days" with a "Buy Now" button fails Authority, Accuracy, and Purpose.

Main Topic 1 • Competency 23 • Smart Search Techniques

Refining Searches with Boolean Operators

What are Boolean Operators? They are simple logical commands written in CAPITAL letters (AND, OR, NOT) that tell medical search engines how to combine or separate your keywords to find exact nursing research articles.

AND (Narrows Search)

Nursing
Safety

Example: Nursing AND Patient Safety
Result: Finds articles containing both terms to narrow your search.

OR (Broadens Search)

Nurse
Nursing

Example: Nurse OR Nursing
Result: Finds articles with either term to expand your options.

NOT (Excludes Terms)

Infection
COVID

Example: Infection NOT COVID-19
Result: Removes unwanted topics to clean up results.

Main Topic 1 • Competency 23 • Clinical Question Formulation

Building Focused Clinical Questions with PICO

What is the PICO Framework? PICO is a framework used in Evidence-Based Nursing to develop clear and focused clinical questions.

P — Population

Who are your patients or participants?
e.g., Nursing students

I — Intervention

What new treatment or action are you testing?
e.g., Video training

C — Comparison

What is the usual or alternative method?
e.g., Standard lecture

O — Outcome

What result or change do you expect?
e.g., Better hand hygiene

Complete PICO Sentence Example:
"In nursing students (P), does video training (I) compared to a standard lecture (C) improve hand hygiene compliance (O)?"

Main Topic 2 • Competency 24 • Study Methodology Selection

Comparing Research Designs

A research design is your overall plan for collecting and analyzing data in your proposal:

Research Design Main Purpose Type of Data Nursing Example
1. Quantitative Measure variables & test cause-and-effect Numbers & stats Counting students following handwashing steps
2. Qualitative Understand human feelings & lived experiences Words & stories Interviewing nurses about ICU stress
3. Mixed-Methods Get complete picture using both approaches Numbers & words Exam scores (numbers) + student feedback (words)
Main Topic 2 • Competency 24: Develop a Research Proposal

Research Proposal: Definition & 9 Essential Elements

Definition of Research Proposal: A formal, structured written plan prepared before starting a study that explains what clinical problem will be investigated, why it is important, and how data will be collected and protected ethically.

1. TitleClear, concise study title
2. IntroductionClinical context & background
3. Problem StatementSpecific gap in nursing care
4. Research ObjectivesMeasurable study goals
5. Research QuestionsFocused PICO inquiry
6. Literature ReviewSummary of prior evidence
7. MethodologyDesign, sample & tools
8. Ethical ConsiderationsConsent & privacy safeguards
9. ReferencesAccurate APA citations
Proposal Assembly Blueprint COMPETENCY 24
Phase A: Title, Problem & Objectives (1–5) DRAFT
Phase B: Literature & Methodology (6–7) DESIGN
Phase C: Ethics & References (8–9) REVIEW
All 9 sections are assembled prior to Ethics Board submission
Main Topic 2 • Competency 24: Develop a Research Proposal • Step-by-Step Workflow

Steps in Developing a Research Proposal

  • Step 1 — Clinical Problem: Identify a real ward issue (e.g., hand hygiene compliance).
  • Step 2 — Literature Review: Search PubMed & CINAHL for existing studies.
  • Step 3 — Research Question & Design: Choose PICO question, sample, and instrument.
  • Step 4 — Ethical Planning: Prepare informed consent and confidentiality safeguards.
  • Step 5 — Proposal Submission: Submit to faculty & Ethics Review Board for formal approval.

💡 Key Academic Distinction

Proposal Development is drafting your 9-part research blueprint, whereas Formal Ethical Approval is the independent review committee permission required before approaching any participants.

Main Topic 2 • Competency 24 • Protecting Human Participants

Ethical Principles in Nursing Research

🤝 Informed Consent & Privacy

Participants join voluntarily after understanding risks and benefits, can withdraw anytime, and personal identity stays protected.

🔒 Confidentiality

Never publish patient names or IDs. Use secret codes (e.g., Participant #01) to keep personal records private.

⚖️ Beneficence, Non-Maleficence & Justice

Do Good, Do No Harm, and treat every participant fairly and equitably.

📝 Proper Referencing

Never plagiarize. Always write in your own words and cite original sources properly.
