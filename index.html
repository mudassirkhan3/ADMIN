Objective 01

Research Skills in Evidence-Based Nursing

Evidence-Based Practice (EBP) combines scientific research, clinical expertise, and patient preferences to make the best care decisions.

  • Patient Safety: Prevents medical errors and hospital infections using proven protocols.
  • Better Outcomes: Up-to-date wound care and pain management shorten recovery times.
  • Critical Thinking: Teaches nurses to question old habits and verify facts.
Nursing science drives modern patient recovery and safety
Objectives 02 & 03

Classifying Research Data

📊 Primary vs Secondary Data

Primary Data: First-hand information collected directly by the researcher (e.g., surveying patients in a ward about pain levels).

Secondary Data: Existing data collected by someone else (e.g., hospital admission records or published journal articles).

📈 Quantitative vs Qualitative

Quantitative: Numerical data measured with numbers and statistics (e.g., blood pressure readings, temperature, heart rate).

Qualitative: Descriptive words and experiences (e.g., patient interviews describing chronic pain feelings).

Objective 04

Reliable Scientific Databases

When searching for nursing literature, avoid random web searches and use indexed health databases:

  • PubMed: Free U.S. National Library of Medicine database with millions of biomedical studies.
  • CINAHL: The premier database specifically dedicated to nursing and allied health literature.
  • Cochrane Library: The global standard for systematic reviews and clinical trial summaries.
Indexed databases ensure access to peer-reviewed medical journals
Objective 05

Assessing Information with the CRAAP Test

C

Currency

Is the information recent and up-to-date?

R

Relevance

Does it directly fit your clinical question?

A

Authority

Are the authors qualified experts?

A

Accuracy

Is it backed by evidence and citations?

P

Purpose

Is it objective, or trying to sell something?

Objective 06

Refining Searches with Boolean Operators

Boolean operators connect your search keywords to find precisely what you need:

  • AND: Narrows results. (e.g., Diabetes AND Insulin shows articles containing both terms).
  • OR: Broadens results. (e.g., Hypertension OR High Blood Pressure captures synonyms).
  • NOT: Excludes terms. (e.g., Diabetes NOT Type 1 removes unwanted topics).
Boolean logic cuts through database clutter to find exact answers
Objective 07

Constructing Questions with PICO

P

Patient / Population

e.g., ICU patients

I

Intervention

e.g., Oral chlorhexidine

C

Comparison

e.g., Standard saline

O

Outcome

e.g., Reduced pneumonia

Using PICO transforms a vague clinical curiosity into a searchable, answerable research question.

Objective 08

Comparing Research Designs

1. Quantitative Design

Focuses on numbers, measurements, and statistical testing (e.g., randomized controlled trials testing drug dosages).

2. Qualitative Design

Focuses on human experiences, interviews, and deep descriptive text (e.g., living with chronic illness).

3. Mixed-Methods Design

Combines both numerical data and qualitative interviews for a complete picture of a clinical problem.

A proposal is your structured plan before starting research
Objective 09

Steps of a Research Proposal

A research proposal outlines your study plan before data collection begins:

  • Title & Introduction: Clear statement of the clinical problem.
  • Objectives & Literature: What is already known and what gap you are filling.
  • Methodology: Study design, population sample, and data collection tools.
  • Timeline & Budget: Work schedule and resource planning.
Objective 10

Ethical Principles in Nursing Research

🤝 Informed Consent

Participants must voluntarily agree to join after understanding all risks, benefits, and their right to withdraw anytime without penalty.

🔒 Confidentiality & Privacy

Protecting participant identities by coding data so personal medical records and responses are never exposed.

⚖️ Beneficence & Safety

Ensuring the study maximizes benefits while preventing any physical or psychological harm to human subjects.

📝 Proper Referencing

Giving full credit to original authors using APA or Vancouver formatting to avoid plagiarism.
