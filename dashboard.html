Unit II: Antimicrobials Rotates Daily
Antifungal Azoles Suffix & Nursing Rule
Suffix: -CONAZOLE = CYP450 Inhibitors
Mechanism: Blocks ergosterol synthesis in fungal cell walls.
Interaction: Increases plasma levels of Warfarin, Statins, & Oral Hypoglycemics.
⚠️ Nursing Priority: Always monitor LFTs (Liver Function Tests) and prothrombin time/INR if co-administered with Warfarin.
